Contact

One address, every inquiry: bisvhackathon@gmail.com. Every message is read by a student organizer, not a form or a queue.

Writing to us

Students and parents: the date, cost, teams, food, and what to bring are covered on the home page and the questions page. Registration for 2027 is open on the registration page.

Companies: put your company name in the subject line, and we will send the 2027 partnership prospectus. Who we partner with, and why, is on the sponsors page.

Judges, speakers, and mentors: applications open soon. Until then, write to us with a short note on what you have built.

Elsewhere

Announcements, help, and team-finding happen on our Discord. We also post on Instagram.
