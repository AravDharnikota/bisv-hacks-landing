About BISV Hacks

What is BISV Hacks?

BISV Hacks is an inclusive, student-led hackathon organized by students at BASIS Independent Silicon Valley Upper School in San Jose, CA. BISV Hacks 2027 will bring high school hackers together for a 12-hour day of building, learning, and collaboration.

Our Mission

Our event encourages creativity and collaboration by welcoming students of all experience levels. Participants can form teams or work solo to develop projects, solve challenges, and gain experience.

What to Expect

In addition to coding, attendees can expect industry speakers, hands-on workshops, mentorship, meals, snacks, and space to turn ideas into working demos.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is BISV Hacks 2027? +

BISV Hacks 2027 is planned for February 2027. We will update this site as soon as the exact date and registration details are ready.

Who can participate? +

Any high school student with a passion for technology can participate, even with no prior coding experience.

Do I need a team? +

You can come solo or work with up to 4 teammates.

Where will the hackathon be held? +

We expect to host BISV Hacks at BASIS Independent Silicon Valley Upper School, located at 1290 Parkmoor Ave, San Jose, CA 95126.

What should I bring? +

Bring a laptop, charger, and anything else you need to build comfortably for the day.

Will there be food? +

Yes. We plan to provide meals, snacks, and refreshments throughout the day.

How can I sponsor, judge, speak, or mentor? +

Use the sponsor prospectus or contact links on this page. We would love to hear from partners, judges, speakers, and mentors for 2027.
