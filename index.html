About

BISV Hacks is a student-led hackathon organized by students at BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, the #3 ranked STEM school in the US. For one day in February, 300+ high school builders come together in San Jose to design and build projects that matter to them, with workshops, mentors, and meals covered.

You do not need coding experience. You can come alone or with up to four teammates. Demand has outgrown the room every year: nearly 200 registrations for 80 spots in 2025, and 200+ registrations that closed in under two months for 2026. For 2027 we have a bigger venue.
