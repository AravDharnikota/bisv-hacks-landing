# Contact

One address, every inquiry: [bisvhackathon@gmail.com](mailto:bisvhackathon@gmail.com). Every message is read by a student organizer, not a form or a queue.

## Writing to us

Students and parents: the date, cost, teams, food, and what to bring are covered on the [home page](https://bisvhacks.com/) and the [questions page](https://bisvhacks.com/faq). Registration for 2027 is open on the [registration page](https://bisvhacks.com/register).

Companies: put your company name in the subject line, and we will send the 2027 partnership prospectus. Who we partner with, and why, is on the [sponsors page](https://bisvhacks.com/sponsors).

Judges, speakers, and mentors: applications open soon. Until then, write to us with a short note on what you have built.

## Elsewhere

Announcements, help, and team-finding happen on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/T8cuesaNKB). We also post on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/bisv.hacks/).

BISV Hacks is organized at BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, 1290 Parkmoor Ave, San Jose, CA 95126. The 2027 venue may change, and any change will be posted here first.
