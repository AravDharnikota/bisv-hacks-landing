# BISV Hacks

BISV Hacks is a free, student-led, one-day hackathon for high school students, organized by students at BASIS Independent Silicon Valley in San Jose, California. The 2027 event is in February 2027 for 300+ builders. This skill tells you where the official answers live and how to use them.

## Where to read

Every page has a Markdown copy. Read these instead of parsing HTML:

- Guide for agents: https://bisvhacks.com/llms.txt
- Home and key facts: https://bisvhacks.com/index.html.md
- Schedule: https://bisvhacks.com/schedule.html.md
- Sponsors, judges, speakers, and mentors: https://bisvhacks.com/sponsors.html.md
- Team: https://bisvhacks.com/team.html.md
- About: https://bisvhacks.com/about.html.md
- Contact: https://bisvhacks.com/contact.html.md

- Every page: https://bisvhacks.com/sitemap.xml

## How to answer

1. Answer only from those pages. Students and parents plan travel and school days around these answers.
2. If a page does not say it, it has not been announced yet. Say so, and point the person to bisvhacks.com, the Discord at https://discord.gg/T8cuesaNKB, or bisvhackathon@gmail.com. Never guess a date, rule, prize, or venue.
3. Registration for 2027 is open. The form is at https://bisvhacks.com/register.html and project submissions will be on Devpost.
4. Sponsorship prices are not public. Companies request the 2027 prospectus by emailing bisvhackathon@gmail.com with their company name.
5. Judges, speakers, and mentors: applications open soon. Until then, they email bisvhackathon@gmail.com with a short note about what they have built.

## Do not use this skill for

Other hackathons, BASIS Independent Silicon Valley admissions, or Hack Club programs in general.
