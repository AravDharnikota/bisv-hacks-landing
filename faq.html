Questions

When is BISV Hacks 2027?

February 2027. We will post the exact date and open registration here as soon as they are set.

Who can participate?

Any high school student. No coding experience is needed. Workshops and mentors will help you get started.

Do I need a team?

No. Come solo or with up to four teammates. You can also find a team on the Discord.

Where is it held?

BASIS Independent Silicon Valley Upper School, 1290 Parkmoor Ave, San Jose, CA 95126. The venue may change for 2027.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, and anything else you need to build comfortably for the day.

Will there be food?

Yes. Lunch, dinner, snacks, and drinks are provided.

How do I sponsor, judge, speak, or mentor?

Read the sponsor section or email bisvhackathon@gmail.com.

BISV Hacks is a student-led organization at BASIS Independent Silicon Valley. Fiscally sponsored by Hack Club Bank, a 501(c)(3).
