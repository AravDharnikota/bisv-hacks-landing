# Sponsors

Build anything: agents, backends, apps, voice, payments, and data.

BISV Hacks puts professional AI and developer tools in the hands of the people who will build with them next. This February, we are expanding to 300+ high school builders from across the Bay Area, each taking an idea to a working demo in twelve hours.

We partner with a careful set of companies each year who align with our mission: AI, agent, and developer platforms whose APIs and SDKs are worth building on, from early-stage startups to the largest platforms. If your platform makes building faster, more powerful, and open to more people, we want teams building on it from the first hour.

We prioritize cash partnerships, which fund the venue, meals, and prizes. We also welcome product credits that align with our mission.

The 2027 prospectus is shared by request. Tell us your company and we will send it over.

## Judges, speakers, and mentors

The people in the room shape what our builders believe is possible. Each year we invite a small group of people who have built real things to spend the day with us.

We are looking for founders who have taken a company from an idea to real users, senior engineers and technical leads who have shipped products used by millions, researchers working at the frontier of AI and agents, investors and operators who have watched hundreds of products succeed and fail, and designers and product leaders who know the difference between a demo that works and a demo that lands.

Applications for judges, speakers, and mentors open soon. We review every application by hand, and the 2027 lineup will be announced here.
